Confirmed:

- Eligibility: FAQ says business or individual creators can sign up, and anyone who can share links through their own homepage, blog, SNS, or channel can start.
- Attribution: purchases within 24 hours after a Sharelink click are counted; last-click wins.
- Rate: public docs state 10% as 5% base + 5% promotion. The readme says the 10% event runs until 2026-09-25, so it must be treated as a time-limited campaign, not a permanent rate.
- Exclusions: self-purchase, family/acquaintance cross-purchase, restricted products, and a Toss Shopping seller's own store products do not count.
- Settlement: monthly revenue is paid on the 25th of the following month after confirmed purchases and tax-invoice requirements. From 2026-06 the minimum payout threshold is 10 won, while older FAQ/payment wording still contains references to the former 10,000 won threshold or carry-over language; use the current 10 won rule but keep the inconsistency visible.
- Tax/business handling: business creators with tax invoice flow must approve Toss-issued reverse tax invoices through Popbill; personal/no-tax-invoice type is paid after withholding as business income.
- Disclosure: every Sharelink post needs economic-interest disclosure. Policy recommends placing the wording in the title or first part, clearly visible in the post.
- Prohibited activity: impersonation/IP misuse, automatic execution or invalid clicks, floating banners, unauthorized third-party content use, adult/illegal media, unsolicited messenger/SMS ads, irrelevant comment posting, and false/exaggerated advertising.
- API-specific guardrail: Open API product `productUrl` is not tracked; posts must use the issued `shortUrl` or `originUrl`.
