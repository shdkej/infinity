Recommended Boundary Copy

UseDraft wording
Agent may readAgents may read Virtue's public pages and public repository documentation to summarize the product, implementation context, and published status.
Agent must not inferAgents must not infer customer authorization, operational readiness, pricing, legal/privacy commitments, or permission to perform actions unless those details are explicitly published and current.
Human handoffWhen a request involves account-specific data, commitments, integrations, spending, deployment, or policy interpretation, hand off to a human Virtue owner for confirmation.
