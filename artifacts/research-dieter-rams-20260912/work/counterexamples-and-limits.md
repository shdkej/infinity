# Limits and counterexamples

## The central translation error

“Less” becomes harmful when it means less *information* rather than less *friction*. W3C requires visible labels/instructions for user input and at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal text at AA. A label-free form, faint copy, icon-only destructive action, or invisible status may look restrained while failing Rams’s own “understandable” and “honest” tests.

## Digital-specific conflict

Physical products usually have bounded functions; software can change state, collect data, and create recurring financial commitments. The FTC documents interfaces that hide terms, impede cancellation, or steer consent. Therefore visual quietness cannot establish honesty: the decisive question is whether choice and consequence are equally discoverable.

## Decision boundary

Use Rams as a **constraint hierarchy** (purpose → clarity → durable system → appearance), not as a style recipe. Do not use it to justify blank space, monochrome, tiny labels, removal of navigation, or generic “minimalism.”
