## 1. Phone Anti-Theft Strap / Tether

### Subtypes

| subtype | ko name | connector method | compatibility risk | option complexity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Wrist strap + universal clip | 손목 스트랩 (범용 클립) | clip onto phone corner notch or case hole | MEDIUM — needs notch/hole or special case | LOW — 1–2 sizes, 2–3 colors |
| B. Crossbody / neck strap | 크로스바디 / 목걸이 스트랩 | clasp onto phone case lug or magsafe ring | HIGH — case compatibility varies | MEDIUM — length, material, connector type |
| C. Tag-holder patch + strap | 패치+스트랩 세트 | adhesive/stick-on patch on phone back | MEDIUM — adhesion quality, residue risk | LOW — mostly single-size |
| D. Security cable / tether | 보안 케이블 / 스트랩 | cable loop around phone body or case | LOW — near-universal | VERY LOW |

### Sourcing Channels (1688 / AliExpress)

- MOQ: 50–200 units per colorway (wrist strap subtype A/D)
- Unit cost: approximately 700–2,500 KRW equivalent (varies by material: nylon < leather < chain)
- Shipping: 7–14 days air freight standard from Chinese suppliers; domestic distributors exist at 1.5–2× markup
- Sample lead time: 7–10 days typical

### Claim/Return Patterns (public review research)

- **Subtype A (wrist strap + clip)**: common complaints are connector clip bending/breaking after repeated use, strap length preference ("너무 짧아요"), color difference from listing photo. Manageable with supplier QA.
- **Subtype B (crossbody)**: higher claim volume — case incompatibility is the top complaint ("케이스에 안 맞아요", "고리가 끊어져요"). High return risk unless bundled with a compatible case.
- **Subtype C (patch + strap)**: adhesive failure complaints ("3일 만에 떨어져요"), residue on phone. Risk is reputational; harder to QA pre-shipment.
- **Subtype D (security cable)**: fewest compatibility claims; main complaint is visual bulk ("줄이 너무 두꺼워요", "거슬려요"). Niche positioning but very low claim risk.

### User-Fit Assessment

- Travel / pickpocket-prevention angle: ★★★ — matches user's travel-memory / loss-prevention identity
- Content angle: natural for "혼자 여행할 때 필수템" or "유럽여행 도난방지 세트" copy
- Knowledge Lab signal: `Idea/Travel.md` references travel security; adjacent to travel documentation theme

### Sourcing-Friction Verdict (Phone Strap)

| subtype | friction | claim risk | user fit | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Wrist strap + universal clip | LOW | LOW–MEDIUM | ★★★ | **GO — primary entry subtype** |
| B. Crossbody strap | MEDIUM | HIGH | ★★★ | WATCH (hold until case compatibility solved) |
| C. Patch + strap | MEDIUM | MEDIUM–HIGH | ★★ | HOLD |
| D. Security cable/tether | VERY LOW | LOW | ★★ | WATCH (bundle component candidate) |

**Recommendation:** Enter with Subtype A (범용 클립 손목 스트랩). Target 2–3 colorways (black, beige, khaki). Differentiate with "유럽여행/혼여 도난방지" copy angle. Avoid crossbody as lead until case-compatibility sourcing is solved.

## 2. Compression / Packing Pouch

### Subtypes

| subtype | ko name | positioning | option complexity | competition density |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Roll-up compression bag | 롤업 압축팩 / 압축 파우치 | reduce luggage volume by rolling air out | HIGH (size S/M/L, material, valve vs. roll) | VERY HIGH — dominated by large volume sellers |
| B. Vacuum compression bag | 진공 압축팩 | pump/vacuum out air for maximum compression | HIGH (size, valve type, pump included or not) | HIGH — also crowded, but more size-differentiated |
| C. Laundry / wet-clothes bag | 세탁물 파우치 / 젖은 옷 주머니 | separate dirty/wet clothes in luggage | LOW (waterproof, size) | LOW — but thin demand signal |
| D. Packing cube | 패킹큐브 | organize, not compress | MEDIUM (size set, material) | MEDIUM — growing category |

### Sourcing Channels

- Roll-up: abundant on 1688, MOQ 50–100. Unit cost ~1,000–3,000 KRW. High volume sellers = price war risk.
- Wet bag: MOQ 100+. Unit cost ~1,500–2,500 KRW. Less competitive.
- Packing cube: higher unit cost (~3,000–7,000 KRW), more tangible quality differentiation possible.

### Claim/Return Patterns

- **Subtype A (roll-up)**: top complaints — "한번 쓰고 압축이 안 됨", "비닐 냄새", "뚜껑이 열려요". Durability is the #1 vulnerability. Supplier QA on zipper/valve quality is critical.
- **Subtype B (vacuum)**: pump complaints, size mismatch ("이불용인데 옷이 너무 작아요"). High-barrier for a new seller.
- **Subtype C (wet bag)**: few complaints, but very low volume. HOLD until demand signal improves.
- **Subtype D (packing cube)**: complaints about zipper quality and size accuracy. Generally more forgiving than compression.

### User-Fit Assessment

- Travel organization: ★★ — functional but doesn't strongly align with user's travel-memory or daily-system identity
- Content angle: serviceable ("짐 효율" or "파우치 패킹") but less emotionally resonant than strap
- Prior screen: compression pouch was rated WATCH due to crowded textile/option burden

### Sourcing-Friction Verdict (Compression Pouch)

| subtype | friction | claim risk | user fit | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Roll-up compression bag | HIGH (options/competition) | HIGH (durability) | ★★ | WATCH — defer until phone strap is validated |
| B. Vacuum compression bag | HIGH | MEDIUM | ★★ | HOLD |
| C. Wet/laundry bag | LOW | LOW | ★★ | HOLD (thin demand) |
| D. Packing cube | MEDIUM | MEDIUM | ★★ | WATCH (parallel track candidate) |

**Recommendation:** Do NOT escalate compression pouch to approval yet. If phone strap Subtype A validates well, revisit compression/packing cube as a **bundle component** (strap + small packing cube = "여행 도난방지+정리 세트"). Standalone compression-bag lead has too much option/QC burden for a new store.
