## Phone Strap / Tether Subtypes

### A. 손목 스트랩 (Wrist strap) — WATCH LEAD

- **Form**: Short cord (30–50cm) with loop-through or carabiner clip; attaches to phone case strap hole or via universal adapter
- **Korea terms**: 핸드폰 손목줄, 핸드폰 손목 고정 스트랩, 여행용 손목스트랩
- **Sourcing**: China direct (1688/AliExpress) — nylon, paracord, leather options widely available; MOQ 50–100 units; unit cost 500–2,000 KRW
- **Retail price**: 5,000–12,000 KRW
- **Option complexity**: LOW — color (2–3 options), material (nylon/paracord/leather), length (one-size vs adjustable)
- **Compatibility risk**: MEDIUM — needs strap hole in case; universal clip adapter (~2,000 KRW) resolves this; address in listing description with clear case compatibility guidance
- **Claim risk**: LOW — "손목 고정" / "소매치기 방지" are descriptive and defensible for travel context; avoid quantitative safety guarantees
- **Primary return driver**: "내 케이스에 스트랩 구멍이 없어요" → mitigated by bundling universal adapter or prominent disclaimer
- **QA focus**: clasp/closure durability, cord tensile strength (target 5kg pull test)
- **Travel positioning**: 유럽 여행 소매치기 방지 손목줄 — matches SearchAd buyer intent (핸드폰도난방지스트랩 4.26% mobile CTR)
- **Verdict**: **WATCH LEAD** — lowest friction profile, strongest SearchAd signal, manageable return risk

### B. 크로스바디/목걸이 스트랩 (Crossbody / neck strap) — WATCH

- **Form**: Longer strap (80–130cm) worn over shoulder or neck; phone hangs at chest or hip
- **Korea terms**: 핸드폰 목걸이줄, 스마트폰 크로스백 스트랩
- **Sourcing**: China — polyester/nylon webbing; MOQ 100–200 units; unit cost 1,000–3,500 KRW
- **Retail price**: 10,000–22,000 KRW
- **Option complexity**: MEDIUM — strap width (1cm/2cm), length (fixed vs adjustable), connector type (carabiner, spring hook, swivel clip), material
- **Compatibility risk**: MEDIUM-HIGH — universal connector adapter required; phone size variance affects clip stability
- **Claim risk**: LOW-MEDIUM — avoid quantified "방지 효과" percentages
- **Primary return driver**: "길이 조절이 불편해요", "커넥터가 흔들려요"
- **QA focus**: swivel clip load rating, adjustable buckle durability
- **Verdict**: **WATCH** — solid secondary SKU; consider as bundle companion to wrist strap

### C. 태그 홀더 패치 + 스트랩 세트 (Adhesive patch + strap set) — HOLD

- **Form**: Adhesive disc/ring adhered to phone back or case; strap clips onto ring; doubles as kickstand
- **Korea terms**: 핸드폰 핑거링 스트랩, 핸드폰 고리 줄 세트
- **Sourcing**: China — widely available; MOQ 100–200 units
- **Retail price**: 8,000–18,000 KRW
- **IP risk**: HIGH — PopSocket LLC holds internationally-registered design/utility patents covering expanding disc + mounting base mechanism; functional equivalents exist but legal exposure non-trivial; requires patent clearance before sourcing
- **Adhesive return risk**: HIGH — "접착력이 약해요", "2주 만에 떨어졌어요" are predictable complaints in this category; difficult to QA at scale
- **Verdict**: **HOLD** — IP barrier + adhesive quality return risk make this a poor lead SKU

## Compression / Packing Pouch Subtypes

### A. 압축파우치 — 핸드롤/진공 압축 (Vacuum / roll-compression bag) — HOLD as lead

- **Form**: Zippered bag; compress by rolling out air or via valve; typically sold as set of 3–4 sizes
- **Korea terms**: `압축파우치`, `여행용압축파우치` (SearchAd confirmed: 1,230 PC + 7,020 mobile/mo — very high volume)
- **Sourcing**: Very easy; extreme factory competition; MOQ 100–300 units; unit cost 800–2,500 KRW/piece
- **Retail price**: 5,000–25,000 KRW (set)
- **Option complexity**: HIGH — sizes (S/M/L/XL), compression mechanism (zipper+valve vs double-zipper vs roll-top), material (PVC vs nylon vs Oxford cloth)
- **Competition**: Very crowded — top-10 sellers have thousands of reviews; white-label commodity with almost no differentiation headroom
- **Claim risk**: MEDIUM — "60% 공간 절약" 류의 수치 주장 → 소비자 기대 vs. 실제 격차 발생 가능
- **Return risk**: VERY HIGH — known high-complaint category: "압축이 잘 안 돼요", "공기가 새요", "사진이랑 달라요"
- **QA focus**: valve seal integrity, zipper durability, material thickness — difficult to verify consistently at low unit cost
- **Verdict**: **HOLD as standalone lead** — demand volume is real but return burden + competition intensity too high for a new seller

### B. 패킹큐브 세트 (Packing cube set) — WATCH

- **Form**: Zippered fabric cubes (3–4 sizes) for organizing clothes inside suitcase; NOT vacuum compression
- **Korea terms**: `패킹큐브`, `여행 짐 정리 큐브` — SearchAd validation pending (next action)
- **Sourcing**: Easy — nylon/polyester; MOQ 100–200 sets; unit cost 3,000–8,000 KRW/set
- **Retail price**: 15,000–35,000 KRW (set of 3–4)
- **Option complexity**: MEDIUM — color, material (mesh-top panel vs solid), size set configuration
- **Competition**: Moderate — established brands (domestic + import) present but mid-tier white-label has room
- **Claim risk**: LOW — "짐 정리 시스템" is unambiguous and testable
- **Return risk**: LOW — physical inspection easy; product does what it says; main QA point is zipper quality
- **Differentiation angle**: "여행 동선별 큐브 분류 시스템" — fits user's travel/documentation content rhythm naturally
- **Verdict**: **WATCH** — better friction profile than vacuum compression; preferred compression-space entry

### C. 세탁물 분리 파우치 (Laundry / wet clothes separator pouch) — WATCH (bundle)

- **Form**: Waterproof zippered bag for separating dirty/wet clothes from clean items during travel
- **Korea terms**: `세탁물파우치` (very thin SearchAd: <10 PC + 20 mobile/mo standalone), but strong as bundle
- **Sourcing**: Very easy — waterproof taffeta, PEVA, or TPU silicone; MOQ 100–200 units; very low unit cost
- **Retail price**: 5,000–12,000 KRW
- **Option complexity**: LOW — size, color
- **Claim risk**: VERY LOW — simple waterproof bag claim
- **Return risk**: VERY LOW — simple, well-understood utility; almost no expectation gap
- **Bundle fit**: Strong companion to packing cube or wrist strap; "젖은 수영복 + 세탁물 분리" is a natural travel-story purchase
- **Verdict**: **WATCH as bundle component** — very low standalone demand but excellent bundle filler
