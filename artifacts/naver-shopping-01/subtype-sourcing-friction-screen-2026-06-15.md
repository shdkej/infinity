## Phone Strap / Tether Subtypes

### 1. 크로스바디/넥스트랩 — APPROVED FOR NEXT SCREEN

**Subtype definition**: Adjustable neck/shoulder strap with universal case attachment (card slot connector, loop-through connector). Strap length 60-120cm, worn crossbody under jacket or visible.

**소싱 마찰 (낮음)**
- 1688/Alibaba: abundant SKUs at $0.80-2.50 per unit
- MOQ: 50-100 units no-brand; 100-200 units for branded hardware
- Lead time: 15-25 days (existing color/length variants), 25-40 days (custom hardware)
- Sample cost: $5-15 for 1-3 samples
- Korean SmartStore context: active sellers including 지가드(Gigard) brand at 13,800-26,800 KRW; accessible market entry confirmed

**클레임 위험 (관리 가능)**
- "Universal compatibility" claim manageable if description specifies "카드슬롯/고리홀 장착" and avoids "전기종 호환" without caveats
- Recommended attachment: card slot holder or loop-through (NOT adhesive pad — adhesive generates complaints)
- "도난방지" framing legitimate for pickpocket-risk travel context (Europe, crowded cities)
- Do NOT claim electronic alarm/active security

**반품 위험 (낮음-중간)**
- Main trigger: carabiner/clip hardware failure (cheap zinc alloy breaks under pull stress)
- Mitigation: specify aluminum alloy carabiner; show load test in listing photos
- Secondary trigger: card slot holder slips out; mitigation: reinforce clip depth spec
- Expected return rate: ~3-8% with aluminum carabiner vs. ~15-20% with zinc

**옵션 복잡도 (낮음)**
- Viable range: 2-3 colors (black, beige/tan, olive) × 1 adjustable length (60-120cm)
- Total SKUs: 2-3 → manageable for solo operator
- Do not expand to full color range at launch

**마진 플로어**
- Sourcing: ~1,200-2,500 KRW per unit (including packaging)
- Selling price: 12,000-20,000 KRW (comparable to Gigard 13,800-26,800 KRW range)
- Gross margin: ~75-85% before platform fees
- Net margin after Naver fees (~10-12%): ~60-72%

**콘텐츠 각도**
- "유럽 소매치기 방지 여행 필수템" (Europe pickpocket-prevention travel essential)
- User can naturally connect to travel narrative
- Lifestyle photo: strap worn crossbody in busy market/museum setting

**VERDICT: 다음 게이트 진행** — 소싱 인콰이어리 + 샘플 주문 계획 수립 단계

### 2. 손목 스트랩 (Wrist Strap) — WATCH, 번들 전용

**Subtype definition**: Simple wrist loop attached to phone case via card slot or ring mount. Short (20-25cm loop).

- 소싱 마찰: 매우 낮음 — 1688 $0.20-0.60/unit, MOQ 50 units
- 클레임 위험: 낮음 — product does what it says (prevents drops)
- 반품 위험: 매우 낮음
- 옵션 복잡도: 매우 낮음
- **문제**: ASP 4,000-8,000 KRW → 절대 마진 너무 낮음. 단독 리드 불가.

**VERDICT**: 번들 전용 부품 (크로스바디 스트랩 주문 시 저비용 번들 추가 가능)

---

### 3. 태그홀더 + 스트랩 세트 (Tag Holder Patch + Strap Bundle) — WATCH, 복잡

**Subtype definition**: Adhesive or stick-on loop/patch on phone back + detachable strap clip. High-end "clean look" without card slot.

- 소싱 마찰: 중간 — fewer quality suppliers on 1688; MOQ often 200+ units; adhesive quality varies
- 클레임 위험: 중간-높음 — adhesive failure (patch falls off) is primary complaint
- 반품 위험: 중간 — heat-resistant adhesive spec required
- 옵션 복잡도: 중간 — patch design × strap color × attachment styles = multiple SKUs

**VERDICT**: WATCH. More complex than crossbody strap. Consider only after crossbody strap is established.

## Compression Pouch Subtypes

### 1. 여행용 압축 파우치 세트 (Travel Compression Cube Set) — HOLD

**Subtype definition**: 3-4 piece zipper compression cube set in nylon/polyester, S/M/L sizes.

**경쟁 과밀**:
- Branden Seoul: "한국 No.1 오거나이저" — strong brand recognition, established community
- Eagle Creek, BÉIS, NOMATIC: global premium players with clear positioning
- `압축파우치` SearchAd ad depth 10 = maximum competition slots filled
- OpenAPI `압축파우치` 175,568 total product count = deep incumbent pool

**클레임 위험 (높음)**:
- "50% 압축" claims commonly disputed; actual compression varies by fabric type and contents
- "방수" claims: most products are water-resistant only (not waterproof), generates complaints
- Size (S/M/L) vs. actual liter capacity confusion = frequent purchase regret

**반품 위험 (높음)**:
- Zipper failure under repeated heavy compression = most common return trigger
- YKK vs. off-brand zipper makes large quality difference (cost impact)
- Expected return rate: 10-20% with commodity sourcing

**옵션 복잡도 (높음)**:
- 3 sizes × 3-5 colors = 9-15 SKUs minimum
- High inventory risk for solo operator with limited capital

**VERDICT: HOLD** — Overcrowded market, established brand competition, high return risk, high option complexity. Not suitable as first lead SKU.

### 3. 하이브리드 파우치 (Travel Organizer + Light Compression) — WATCH, 차별화 필요

**Subtype definition**: Multi-pocket travel organizer with optional compression. Examples: cable organizer pouch with compression, cosmetics + compression, document + compression.

- 소싱 마찰: 중간
- 클레임 위험: 중간 (less aggressive compression claims, but "방수" still present)
- 반품 위험: 중간
- 옵션 복잡도: 중간-높음
- **Differentiation potential**: If bundled with user's travel narrative (포토/크리에이터 여행 오거나이저 세트) could stand out, but needs clear story

**VERDICT**: WATCH. Not ready until crossbody strap is established and differentiation angle is defined.

## Recommended Next Actions

1. **크로스바디/넥스트랩** → 소싱 인콰이어리 단계 진입:
   - 1688 공급자 2-3개 선별 (aluminum carabiner, card slot attachment, 60-120cm adjustable)
   - 샘플 요청: 블랙, 베이지, 올리브 × 1 length variant
   - 리스팅 설명 템플릿 초안 (카드슬롯 방식, 정확한 길이 범위, 소매치기 방지 프레이밍, 접착식 제외, "전기종" 클레임 없음)
   
2. **압축 파우치** → WATCH 유지:
   - 크로스바디 스트랩 소싱 진행 중 병렬 불가
   - 사용자가 명확한 차별화 각도 제시 시에만 재검토

3. **손목 스트랩** → 크로스바디 스트랩 첫 주문 시 저비용 번들 추가 옵션으로만 고려
