# Central Europe 3 Cities Title Research

- id: marketing-119
- date: 2026-07-27
- owner: SAM + marketing agent
- scope: Threads title/front-line candidates for Prague, Vienna, Budapest travel content.

## Context

The user liked the structure around `중앙유럽 3도시를 지나며 느낀 차이`, but rejected the leading labels:

- `기내수하물 장기여행자`: awkward person label.
- `40L 여행`: too forced.
- `미니멀 여행가`: bad fit.
- `기내수하물만 들고`: still weak.
- `세계여행 중`: natural but too general and not curiosity-driving.

Working title target: keep the central-europe three-city comparison, but make the hook come from contrast, changed criteria, or unexpected feeling rather than luggage identity.

## Search And Market Signals

Naver DataLab and SearchAd checks:

- `기내용캐리어` has strong search volume, but leads the content toward product/search-shopping intent rather than a travel reflection.
- `여행가방` also has search volume, but title-led use feels like a packing post.
- `여행준비물` is stable, but too generic for a Threads first line.
- `기내수하물`, `짐줄이기`, `미니멀여행`, `가방하나`, `캐리어하나` are either low-volume or awkward as a lead phrase.

Web scan pattern:

- Existing travel content around Prague/Vienna/Budapest tends to use generic list or prep frames: city tips, 야경, 준비물, 캐리어, 짐싸기.
- To avoid blending into generic travel/prep content, the stronger angle is not "small luggage" but "the travel standard changed city by city."

Useful references:

- YouTube/search result examples around Prague and Prague-Budapest-Vienna frequently use broad travel-tip hooks.
- Travel prep results show strong luggage/prep terms, but those frame the post as checklist content rather than the user's account-native travel reflection.

## Marketing Agent Verdict

Marketing agent's strongest direction:

- Do not lead with a person label or product keyword.
- Use city-to-city contrast, changed travel standard, or unexpected feeling.
- Best proposed frame: `프라하에서 빈, 부다페스트로 갈수록 달라진 여행 기준`.

Marketing agent ranked candidates:

1. `가볍게 움직이며 중앙유럽 3도시를 지나며 느낀 차이`
2. `도시를 옮길수록 중앙유럽 3도시에서 다르게 보인 것`
3. `프라하에서 빈, 부다페스트로 갈수록 달라진 여행 기준`
4. `중앙유럽 3도시를 지나며 생각보다 달랐던 것들`
5. `같은 중앙유럽인데 프라하·빈·부다페스트는 꽤 달랐다`

SAM adjustment:

- Candidate 1 is still a little soft because `가볍게 움직이며` does not create enough curiosity.
- Candidate 3 is strongest because it names the actual route and promises a changing standard, not just a feeling.
- Candidate 2 is usable as a softer Threads opener, but weaker for a title.

## Final Recommendation

Final pick:

`프라하에서 빈, 부다페스트로 갈수록 달라진 여행 기준`

Why:

- It is specific: the cities and movement order are visible.
- It creates curiosity: what standard changed?
- It avoids awkward identity labels.
- It matches the user's content taste: choice/standard/actual experience rather than broad travel sentiment.
- It can naturally include luggage/minimal travel in the body without forcing it into the title.

Backup candidates:

1. `프라하, 빈, 부다페스트를 지나며 여행 기준이 바뀌었습니다`
2. `도시를 옮길수록 중앙유럽이 다르게 보였습니다`
3. `같은 중앙유럽인데 프라하·빈·부다페스트는 꽤 달랐습니다`
4. `중앙유럽 3도시를 지나며 생각보다 달랐던 것들`

## Draft Use

Recommended Threads first line:

> 프라하에서 빈, 부다페스트로 갈수록 여행 기준이 조금씩 바뀌었습니다.

Follow-up body direction:

- Prague: beautiful, but tiring because beauty pulls crowds.
- Vienna: expensive, so taste and priority become visible faster.
- Budapest: more about recovery than sightseeing.
- Luggage/minimal point: mention later as a quiet supporting fact, not as the main title.
