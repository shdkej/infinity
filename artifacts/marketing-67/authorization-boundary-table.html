Virtue AI Authorization Boundary Table

Internal prelaunch strategy artifact translating the WEF and Capgemini agent-control framing into Virtue journey-level authorization boundaries. This is not public copy and does not change product behavior, analytics, privacy posture, deployment, or cost.

axis ax1: Delegation depth. The boundary moves from user-authored drafts toward agent-assisted orchestration, but every step keeps the human decision owner visible.
axis ax2: Evidence burden. The more Virtue acts on behalf of a user, the more it must show source evidence, reversible state, and an explicit confirmation point.

Authorization Boundaries

Journey User delegates Virtue may do Virtue must not do Human decision required Evidence to show
J1
Capture		 Collect raw context, goals, constraints, and unresolved questions from the user or connected workspace. Normalize notes, identify missing context, group related facts, and ask for clarification when confidence is low. Infer consent, invent facts, contact external parties, publish content, or alter source records. Whether the captured intent is accurate enough to become an active brief. Original snippets, source locations, uncertainty labels, and a short list of assumptions.
J2
Shape		 Turn the brief into options, tradeoffs, drafts, target segments, or positioning hypotheses. Compare alternatives, reuse approved learnings, draft internal artifacts, and flag conflicts with prior strategy. Claim market validation, create public promises, change tracking/privacy settings, or choose the strategy unilaterally. Which option becomes the working direction and which claims are allowed. Decision matrix, rejected alternatives, source citations, and known gaps.
J3
Prepare		 Convert the approved direction into launch-ready internal assets, checklists, or implementation prompts. Produce drafts, map dependencies, prepare review packets, and define verification gates for later execution. Deploy, message customers, spend money, create new data collection, or bypass approval gates. Approval to execute, publish, spend, or ask another agent to perform code or operational work. Reviewable artifact, approval checklist, risk register, and explicit go/no-go gates.
J4
Act		 Execute a narrow approved action with a defined scope, rollback path, and success gate. Carry out the authorized local action, verify the result, and record completion evidence. Expand scope, make irreversible business decisions, run unapproved outreach, or hide failure states. Any escalation beyond the approved scope, external visibility, payment, legal/privacy impact, or irreversible change. Before/after state, command or artifact evidence, verification result, rollback note, and completion report.
