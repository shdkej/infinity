## Learning Summary

### Inherited Assumptions

- Guided first value is a four-stage handoff, not a push toward faster output alone.
- First-input defaults steer the job.
- Decision-delegation risk rides the verb; control is preserved through affordances.

### Changed Assumptions

- None. This intent narrows the earlier four-stage guided-first-value frame to the first-input prompt-design surface.

### Conflicts

- No conflict with `marketing-32`, `marketing-45`, or `marketing-51`.

### Reusable Rule

AI first-input prompt design should teach the AI the user's desired result, not teach the user the UI and not invite the AI to decide for the user. Click-tax reduction must not remove the reflective input that creates Virtue's value.
